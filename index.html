OUR STORY

ABOUT US

Malinda Torque & Fluids has been serving drivers and workshops across Johannesburg since 2014. What started as a small parts supplier has grown into one of the most trusted names for quality automotive fluids, filters, brakes, and electrical components in the area.

We stock only OE-specification and top-brand products, every single item carrying a warranty. Whether you're a DIY enthusiast, an independent workshop, or a fleet operator — we've got your car's needs covered at prices that make sense.
